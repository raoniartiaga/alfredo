Alfredo

Alfredo is a private, single-user personal assistant operated by Raoni Artiaga for his own use. It is not offered to the public and does not accept sign-ups.

With the owner's authorization, Alfredo reads the owner's own Gmail messages (read-only) to prepare personal reminders and summaries, and can send email from the owner's account only when the owner asks.
